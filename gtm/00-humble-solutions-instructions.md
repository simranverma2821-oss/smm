# Humble Solutions, House Instructions

This is the guide for anyone who writes or speaks as Humble Solutions. It sits above the GTM files and settles any argument about tone. The positioning lives in `01-what.md`, the audience in `02-who.md`, the reasons people buy in `03-why.md`, and the plan in `04-gtm-instructions.md`. This file is about how all of it should sound and feel.

---

## Start with the name

Humble Solutions carries a promise in the first word. Humble is not small and it is not shy. It means we let the work speak before we do. It means we answer the client's problem before we talk about ourselves. Every sentence we publish, every reply we send, and every proposal we write should feel like it came from a team confident enough to stay quiet about its own cleverness and generous enough to help first. Hold that feeling. It is the whole brand.

## What we build, in one honest breath

We are a product engineering team. We build native Android and iOS apps, cross platform apps in React Native, and web products on React and Node. Our sharpest edge is custom AI integration, where we take the software a company already runs and make it think, sort, answer, and act. We work three ways: as a full build team, as dedicated developers who sit with your people, or as the quiet engineering team behind another agency's brand. That is the truth of us, and we never dress it up beyond what we can deliver.

## Where we are going

We are turning a business built on word of mouth into one that earns every month whether or not the phone rings. That is the goal behind all of this. Recurring revenue, the kind that lets Humble Solutions stand on its own feet. Our first and best audience is small software and digital agencies in the West who win more work than they can build. We become their capacity. One good partner sends work for years. Keep that horizon in view. We are not chasing one more project. We are building relationships that repeat.

## How we sound

Write like a person who knows their craft and has nothing to prove. Warm, plain, and exact. A founder talking to another founder over coffee, not a brochure shouting across a trade show.

A few things that make our writing ours:

Lead with the reader's problem, not our service. People do not care what we do until they believe we understand what is hard for them.

Be specific or be silent. "We built an app that cut a clinic's booking time from ten minutes to one" earns trust. "We deliver quality solutions" earns nothing.

Let short sentences carry the weight. A few of them should land hard. Then let a longer one breathe and explain, so the rhythm never turns flat or mechanical.

Show, do not claim. Instead of saying we are reliable, tell the small true story of the deadline we hit when it was hard. Reliability is proven in the telling, never in the adjective.

Use we and you. Name the reader. Keep it human.

Confidence without noise. We can say what we are good at, plainly. We never inflate, never hype, and never promise what we have not done.

## The rules of the pen

These are firm.

No em dashes, ever. Use a comma, a full stop, or a pair of brackets instead.

No double hyphens standing in for a dash.

No corporate filler. Keep these words and their cousins out: leverage, unlock, elevate, seamless, robust, cutting edge, game changer, revolutionary, synergy, delve, landscape, testament, empower, supercharge, best in class. If a word sounds like it came from a pitch deck, cut it.

No jargon walls for non technical readers, and no dumbing down for technical ones. Match the language to the person in front of you. The What file holds both registers. Use the right one.

One idea per sentence when the point matters. Do not stack three clauses and hope the reader keeps up.

Prefer the concrete noun and the plain verb. Cut adverbs that add nothing. "We fixed it fast" beats "we very quickly resolved the situation."

End with a small, clear next step, never a hard sell. "If this sounds like your problem, tell me about it" opens a door. "Buy now" slams one.

## Trust is not only written, it is done

Our words are only as good as our habits. Reply quickly, because speed reads as respect. Answer the question that was actually asked before offering the thing we sell. Give value in the open and pitch only in private, and only once someone has raised a hand. Never overpromise to win a call, because the first broken promise costs more than the deal was ever worth. When we do not know something, we say so, then we go find out. This is how a humble team becomes the trusted one.

## By the channel, one line each

On LinkedIn, our founders write as themselves, tell real stories, and let Humble Solutions sit quietly in the background as the company behind the person.

In communities, we are the helpful name that answers first and sells never, until the door is opened.

In outreach, we open with something true about them, not something polished about us.

In proposals, we mirror the client's own words back to them, show the shortest clear path to a result, and price with a straight face.

## What we never do

We never send a message that could have been sent to anyone. We never use a word we would not say out loud. We never promise a date we cannot keep. We never let a warm message sit cold for hours. And in any sentence we publish, we never sound like a machine wrote it.
