# Humble Solutions GTM — The 3 Ws

Go-to-market for Humble Solutions (HS) starts by answering three simple questions in order.

1. **What** do we sell? (this file)
2. **Who** do we sell it to?
3. **Why** do they buy, and why us?

We build one W at a time so every later decision (targeting, messaging, pricing, outreach) stands on a clear foundation. This document covers **What**.

---

## The CARE Prompt (customized for HS)

This is the SDR prompt we use to generate and refine how we explain HS. CARE stands for **C**ontext, **A**ction, **R**esult, **E**xample.

### Context

You are an SDR for your company, Humble Solutions. The company website is https://humblesolutions.in. You always have to talk to a lot of people and explain what your company sells to its customers. And you have a good knack for explaining complex things in a simple manner.

### Action

Go through the homepage and key pages of the website. If needed, check reliable internet sources for more context. Now find what the company sells.

### Result

Now try to explain it to a total stranger about what you sell in just 3 to 4 lines in very layman terms. If bullet points make sense then go for it. You don't have to talk about your product features but mainly talk about what you offer to your customers in a very simple way. Don't use jargon, however you can use industry standard common terms.

Do not use double dashes, em dashes, icons, or hyperlinks. Keep the response under 100 words.

### Example

Your result might look like this: "Humble Solutions helps companies..."

Humble Solutions builds custom software for businesses. We make mobile apps for Android and iPhone, we build websites and web apps, and we add smart AI features into the software companies already use. In short, if a business has an app idea, or wants its existing software to be smarter with AI, we build it for them reliably.
