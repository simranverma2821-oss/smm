# Humble Solutions GTM — The "Why"

The third W. **Why** do customers buy from HS?

People do not buy software. They buy the removal of a problem. This file lists the pain points HS solves, so every pitch, ad, and cold message can lead with the customer's problem instead of our tech.

We keep two registers again, because our buyers split into direct clients and agency partners, and their pains are different.

---

## The CARE Prompt (customized for HS)

CARE stands for **C**ontext, **A**ction, **R**esult, **E**xample. This is the pain-point version of the prompt.

### Context

You are an SDR at Humble Solutions (https://humblesolutions.in). Understanding the pain points your company solves helps you connect with prospects and effectively position your solution.

### Action

1. Explore the company's homepage and key pages.
2. Consult reliable internet sources if needed for additional context.
3. Identify the primary customer problems that your company solves based on its offerings.
4. Prioritize pain points by importance to the target customer.

### Result

Deliver a list of pain points with these specifications.

- Number: Up to 4 pain points, ranked by importance (most critical first).
- Clarity: Use sixth grade reading level. Simple, direct language.
- Evidence: Include specific facts, statistics, or numbers when available.
- Format: Present as a numbered list or brief paragraph. No dashes, icons, citations, or hyperlinks. Under 100 words total.

### Example

For a company like Hubcredo: "Hubcredo solves these problems for sales and marketing teams: 1. Slow, manual outreach that wastes 10 plus hours per week. 2. Scattered customer data across multiple tools. 3. Poor campaign tracking that makes it hard to see what is working. 4. Repetitive tasks that prevent teams from focusing on closing deals."

## The "Why" — pain points HS solves

### A. Direct clients (businesses and founders who need software built)

Humble Solutions solves these problems for businesses:

1. They have a software or app idea but no reliable team to build it. Freelancers go silent or deliver poor quality work.
2. Their current software is slow and manual. They want AI to handle the busywork but do not know how to add it.
3. Building for both iPhone and Android at once costs too much and takes too long.
4. Hiring good developers in house is expensive, slow, and hard to manage.

### B. Agency partners (agencies who want HS as their white-label team)

Humble Solutions solves these problems for agencies:

1. They win more projects than their own team can build, so they turn work away.
2. They cannot find an offshore team that delivers good quality on time.
3. They need extra skills like AI, iOS, or Android without hiring full time staff.
4. They want to keep their own brand in front while a reliable team builds behind them.

## How each pain maps to what we sell

| Customer pain | What HS offers | Recurring angle |
|---|---|---|
| No reliable team, freelancers vanish | Full custom build, dedicated developer | Monthly retainer or dedicated dev |
| Software is manual, wants AI | Custom AI integration | AI maintenance and tuning plan |
| Needs iPhone and Android | React Native, or native iOS and Android | App care plan |
| In house hiring is costly | Dedicated developers, staff augmentation | Monthly per developer |
| Agency has overflow work | White-label delivery | Ongoing overflow contract |

The recurring angle column is where MRR lives. Every pain we solve has a monthly version.
